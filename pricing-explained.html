How Pricing Works

Each render plan has a minimum charge and an hourly GPU rate. A real precheck estimates your scene and recommends the smallest suitable plan before you approve the job.

  1. Upload your Blender project.
  2. Run a real GPU precheck to estimate render time.
  3. Review the recommended plan, hourly rate and estimated price.
  4. Confirm your job before full rendering begins.

For normal plans, the final render charge is the greater of the plan minimum or the actual GPU render time multiplied by that plan's hourly rate.

Final price depends on measured usage. Your estimate and applicable plan are shown after project analysis rather than guessed in advance.

Render Plans

Budget

Up to 1.5 GPU hours. $1 minimum, then $1.00 per GPU hour. Lowest-cost option for short renders.

Standard

Up to 4 GPU hours. $3 minimum, then $1.00 per GPU hour. The normal choice for typical projects.

Fast

Up to 8 GPU hours. $6 minimum, then $1.25 per GPU hour. Higher queue priority for longer or more time-sensitive jobs.

Express

Up to 12 GPU hours. $10 minimum, then $1.50 per GPU hour. Highest normal queue priority.

Long Render

For eligible jobs expected to exceed 12 GPU hours. $12 minimum, then $1.00 per GPU hour. Long Render is preemptible between frames and uses a lower-priority queue so large jobs can remain economical.

How Delivery Estimates Are Calculated

Delivery estimates are based on real GPU precheck analysis of your project. This means timing is grounded in actual render behavior rather than rough assumptions.

Factors that affect timing include scene complexity, render settings, frame count, measured GPU runtime and the selected plan.
