How Pricing Works

GPU Render Studio uses a simple Price & Speed model. You choose how quickly you want your project delivered, and the system adjusts scheduling priority and resource allocation behind the scenes.

  1. Upload your Blender project.
  2. Choose Budget, Standard, Fast or Express.
  3. Run a real GPU precheck to estimate delivery time.
  4. Confirm your job before full rendering begins.

Final price and estimated delivery are confirmed after project analysis, not guessed in advance.

Delivery Modes

Budget

Lowest price with more flexible delivery timing. Best for non-urgent jobs where saving cost matters more than speed.

Standard

The balanced option for most Blender projects. Combines predictable delivery with reasonable cost.

Fast

Higher priority for shorter delivery times. A good fit when deadlines matter and you want quicker turnaround.

Express

Fastest available delivery for urgent deadlines. Designed for projects that need top priority in the render queue.

How Delivery Estimates Are Calculated

Delivery estimates are based on real GPU precheck analysis of your project. This means timing is grounded in actual render behavior rather than rough assumptions.

Factors that affect timing include scene complexity, render settings, frame count and the selected delivery mode.

What You Pay For

Before You Confirm

After upload, the system analyzes your project and presents the expected delivery setup. This is where final confirmation happens before the full render job starts.

In other words: upload first, analyze second, confirm with clearer information third.
