How Pricing Works

GPU Render Studio charges $1 per included GPU hour. A real precheck estimates your scene and recommends the smallest render window that fits.

  1. Upload your Blender project.
  2. Run a real GPU precheck to estimate render time.
  3. Review the recommended plan and exact price.
  4. Confirm your job before full rendering begins.

Final price and estimated render time are confirmed after project analysis, not guessed in advance.

How Delivery Estimates Are Calculated

Delivery estimates are based on real GPU precheck analysis of your project. This means timing is grounded in actual render behavior rather than rough assumptions.

Factors that affect timing include scene complexity, render settings, frame count and the selected GPU-time window.
