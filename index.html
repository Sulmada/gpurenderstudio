Pricing

Phase 1 pricing applies to fully automated execution profiles. Prices are per completed job — not per minute.

—

Dynamic pricing discounts apply automatically based on system capacity. The final price is locked at submission time and will not change.

Failed jobs are not charged. One submission equals one execution attempt.

Job Results

  • SUCCESS – Output produced. Job is charged.
  • FAILED – PRESET_VIOLATION – Limits exceeded. Not charged.
  • FAILED – TIMEOUT – Runtime exceeded. Not charged.
  • FAILED – START_FAILED – Execution error. Not charged.

One submission equals one execution attempt. Failed jobs are not retried automatically.
