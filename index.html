Frequently Asked Questions

What is GPU Render Studio?

GPU Render Studio is an automated GPU rendering platform for Blender. You upload a finished scene, run a real GPU timing precheck, choose a pricing slot, and the system renders your project unattended.

Which software is supported?

Currently Blender with Cycles GPU rendering on NVIDIA hardware. Additional engines will be added over time.

How does pricing work?

Pricing is based on execution slots, overflow runtime, and an optional spending CAP. The slot price always covers the base runtime. Overflow is billed hourly after the slot window. CAP limits extra spending only.

What is a CAP?

CAP is a safety limit on extra cost above the slot price. It never stops a job during the base slot window. If reached after the slot is exhausted, the job pauses automatically with status paused_cap.

What happens when a job is paused?

When paused due to CAP, all frames are preserved. No further charges occur. Resume support will be added in a later phase.

What does “real GPU precheck” mean?

Before confirming a job, we render test frames on real GPUs. This measures performance, estimates total runtime, and recommends the correct execution profile.

Can I see progress while rendering?

Yes. The live status page shows frames completed, percent done, ETA, and billing progress.

Do I pay before or after rendering?

Payment is required before downloading final results. This keeps pricing predictable and protects compute resources.

Where are your GPUs located?

All systems are hosted in the European Union, suitable for EU customers and GDPR-aware workflows.

What happens if rendering fails?

Failed jobs are not billed. Partial jobs are charged only for completed runtime. The status page always explains the reason.

How long are my files stored?

Uploaded projects and renders are kept temporarily and cleaned up automatically after a retention period.
