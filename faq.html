Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about pricing, speed options, delivery estimates and automated Blender rendering.

How does pricing work?

Choose between Budget, Standard, Fast or Express. Lower cost modes allow more flexible scheduling, while faster modes receive higher priority.

How accurate are delivery estimates?

Estimated times are based on real GPU precheck analysis of your project before the full render starts.

What file types do you accept?

GPU Render Studio currently accepts finished Blender project files prepared for rendering.

Can I track progress live?

Yes. Every job includes a live status page with progress updates, estimated completion and delivery status.

What happens if rendering fails?

Technical platform-side failures are not billed.

Where are GPUs hosted?

Infrastructure is operated in the European Union with privacy-aware workflows.

When do I pay?

Payment is requested when completed output is ready for delivery, depending on workflow and job type.
