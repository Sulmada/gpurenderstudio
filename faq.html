Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about pricing, measured estimates and automated Blender rendering.

← Back to main site   |   Render Tools →

How does pricing work?

Choose between Budget, Standard, Fast or Express. Each plan includes a different GPU-time window. The precheck recommends a suitable plan and shows its exact price before you confirm.

How accurate are delivery estimates?

Estimated times are based on real GPU precheck analysis of your project before the full render starts.

What file types do you accept?

GPU Render Studio currently accepts finished Blender project files prepared for rendering.

Can I track progress live?

Yes. Every job includes a live status page with progress updates, estimated completion and delivery status.

What happens if rendering fails?

Technical platform-side failures are not billed.

Where are GPUs hosted?

Project processing and render infrastructure are operated in the European Union.

When do I pay?

Uploading and the GPU precheck do not start the paid render. You see the price before approval, and successful jobs are charged according to the plan you confirmed.
