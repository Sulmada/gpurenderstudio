Simple Process

GPU Render Studio is built to make Blender rendering simple: upload your project, choose your preferred speed, receive an estimate, and let the system handle the rest.

Step 1

Upload Your File

Upload your finished Blender project securely through the website.

Step 2

Choose Price & Speed

Select Budget, Standard, Fast or Express depending on cost and delivery priority.

Step 3

Real GPU Precheck

Your project is analyzed using real rendering tests to estimate runtime and expected delivery time.

Step 4

Rendering Begins

Your job runs on high-performance GPUs with automated scheduling and live progress updates.

Step 5

Delivery

When complete, your output is ready for download. Depending on workflow, payment is requested before final delivery.

What Happens Behind the Scenes

The platform automatically manages GPU allocation, queue priority, progress monitoring and delivery handling so you can focus on your project.

You choose the result. The system handles the complexity.
