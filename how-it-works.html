Simple Process

GPU Render Studio is built to make Blender rendering simple: upload your project, receive a measured estimate, approve the suitable plan and let the system handle the rest.

Step 1

Upload Your File

Upload your finished Blender project through the website. This starts the precheck, not the paid render.

Step 2

Real GPU Precheck

Your project is analyzed using a real render timing test to estimate total GPU time.

Step 3

Review and Approve

Review the measured runtime, recommended plan and exact price. The full render begins only after you confirm.

Step 4

Rendering Begins

Your job runs on high-performance GPUs with automated scheduling and live progress updates.

Step 5

Delivery

When complete, your output is ready for download. Successful jobs are charged according to the plan you approved.

What Happens Behind the Scenes

The platform automatically manages GPU allocation, job scheduling, progress monitoring and delivery handling so you can focus on your project.

You choose the result. The system handles the complexity.
