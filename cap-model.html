CAP Spending Model

CAP gives you predictable GPU rendering costs without cutting your guaranteed slot runtime.

← Back to main site

What CAP Is

CAP defines the maximum extra spend allowed after your slot package is consumed. It never replaces the base slot price.

How CAP Works

  • Slot runtime is always guaranteed.
  • Overflow only begins after slot window.
  • CAP limits overflow spend only.
  • Jobs pause safely when CAP is reached.

Example

FLEXIBLE slot: 8h / $4
CAP = $4.50
→ Max total cost: $8.50
→ Approx runtime: 12.5h

Paused Jobs

When CAP is reached the job transitions to paused_cap. Frames are preserved, billing is locked and you may resume later.
